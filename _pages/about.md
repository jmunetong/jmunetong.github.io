Graduate Student at Stanford University in Computational Mathematics and Engineering. My research focuses on generative modeling, efficient transformer architectures, and large-scale ML pipelines. At SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory, I have been fortunate to be advised by Cong Wang and Matthias Kling, developing transformer-based latent diffusion models for high-resolution X-ray diffraction data. Earlier, I was advised by Nikos Vlassis at Adobe Research, where I optimized sub-quadratic self-attention with vector quantization and variational encoding for long-sequence transformers. I also hold a Bachelor of Science in Statistics with Honors from Brown University, where I was advised by Jon Steingrimsson. My background includes ML engineering experience in radar image denoising, adversarial robustness, and scalable data pipelines for production systems.

In my free time, I enjoy reading about AI and ethics, petting dogs, plane spotting, and hiking. My favorite book is Manifesto for a Moral Revolution: Practices to Build a Better World by Jacqueline Novogratz
